01 — Philosophy

More Than A Meal. A Moment Worth Remembering.

NOIR began with a simple belief: that dining is the last true act of attention. Forty seats, one open pass, and a kitchen that edits every plate until only what matters remains.

Philosophy

We cook with restraint. Flavour, texture and memory — in that order. Nothing is on the plate to decorate it.

Hospitality

Service here is felt, not performed. We anticipate, we remember, and we make the room feel like yours.

Ingredients

Small farms, day-boat catch and heritage grains, reaching us within days. The season writes the menu.

    05 — The Chef's World

    Cooking is the art of subtraction.

    Trained in the kitchens of Kyoto, Lyon and Mumbai, Chef Ren brings a disciplined, ingredient-first philosophy to NOIR. Each dish begins with a single question: what can we take away?

    “A great plate is an argument won by subtraction.” — Chef Ren Takahashi
    1. Source

      We know every grower by name. If a farmer cannot tell us when it was picked, it doesn't come in.

    2. Season

      No dish outlives its ingredient. When the first monsoon mangoes end, so does the dessert.

    3. Subtract

      Every plate is edited three times. What remains is only what makes it taste like itself.
